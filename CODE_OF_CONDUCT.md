# Code of conduct

This project is a place to learn. Everyone taking part, as a contributor, user, supervisor or reviewer, is expected to make
it a respectful and welcoming one.

## What we expect

* Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Questions are welcome.
* Disagree with ideas, not with people. Give reasons and evidence.
* Give feedback that is specific and useful, and accept it gracefully.
* Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, languages and ways of working.
* Give credit for other people's work, and do not present it as your own.
* Respect privacy: do not share other people's personal information.

## What is not acceptable

* Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
* Unwelcome sexual attention or sexualised language.
* Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately disrupting discussions.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Passing off fabricated or altered results as real. In this project that is also a breach of the core rule in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, comments, discussions and any place where someone represents the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem to _your contact (replace this placeholder)_. Reports are treated seriously and privately. Maintainers may
edit or remove comments, close discussions, or ask someone to leave the project if their behaviour breaks these rules. For
a student project, serious cases can also be raised with the supervisor or the institution.

This document is a plain-language statement written for this project; it was inspired by common open-source codes of
conduct but is not a copy of any of them.
